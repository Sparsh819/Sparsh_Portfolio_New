About

A chemical engineer who builds things that fly.

I study Chemical Engineering at IIT Madras, and most of my engineering happens in hardware labs and on launch sites. I was the only avionics member in Team Abhyuday’s IREC delegation, coordinated the Aero Club through a national competition season, and spent a summer on vision and guidance for autonomous UAV tracking.

I like owning a system end to end: choosing components, laying out the board, writing the firmware, and then finding out on the pad whether the redundancy was worth it. It was — on our IREC flight, the backup channel on my flight computer fired the drogue when the primary charge failed.
